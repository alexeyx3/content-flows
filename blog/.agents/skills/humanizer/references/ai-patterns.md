# AI patterns in texts for Example Co

A catalogue of editorial defects for EN and RU. The presence of a construction does not
prove AI authorship. The priorities from SKILL.md apply to every example below.

## How to apply the catalogue

- First determine what gets in the reader's way: an empty lead-in, a repeat, an unclear link,
  an unfounded promise. Do not treat a match with the dictionary as sufficient grounds.
- Weak signals — dashes, commas, the passive, formal words, equal length —
  are judged in context. Do not fix them automatically and do not attribute authorship.
- Numbers, real items, conditions, qualifications, negations, the actor and the distribution
  of duties are preserved. "Helps" does not equal "does it for the client".
- The examples show a way of editing. They give no new facts about Example Co.
  Specifics are taken only from the source or from the supplied material.
- If the exact fact is missing, shorten the formula or note the question in the report.
  Do not replace "quickly" with an invented "24 hours", or "may" with a proven result.
- Do not clean quotes, names, technical terms, URLs and protected blocks by the dictionary.

## Universal patterns

### 1. Structural symmetry
Repeated grammar and equal length of theses create rhythm instead of content.
Keep three real functions or three conditions. Do not remove an item for the sake of the number.
Check paragraphs and cards as well, not only sentences.

### 2. Empty intensifiers
seamlessly, effortlessly, powerful, cutting-edge, game-changing; «бесшовно»,
«передовой», «комплексный» with nothing behind them. Delete the word if it specifies nothing.
robust, reliable and other terms in their exact technical sense may be needed.

### 3. Blurred agency
helps, allows, enables, empowers; «позволяет», «помогает», «даёт возможность».
Specify the real action while keeping who performs it.
**Before:** "The platform helps teams manage records by storing agreements together."
**After:** "The platform stores agreements together so teams can manage the records."
You cannot get "The platform manages the contractors for you" out of this sentence.

### 4. Nominalizations instead of action
A pile-up of -tion/-ment and «обеспечение автоматизации оптимизации» hides the action.
**Before:** «Платформа обеспечивает автоматизацию документооборота».
**After:** «Платформа автоматизирует документооборот».
Do not replace a legal name or an exact term with an approximate colloquial word.

### 5. Meta-comments
In summary, It's worth noting, This means that; «Важно отметить», «Подводя итог».
Remove the announcement when the information can be given straight away. Keep a causal link,
a condition or a necessary conclusion: the words «таким образом» ("thus") are not always empty.

### 6. Templated and vague headings
Seamless Contractor Management for Global Teams; «Эффективное решение для бизнеса».
Name the subject of the section and check that it matches the content.
**Before:** "Unlock seamless contractor onboarding".
**After:** "Contractor onboarding steps", if there really are steps inside.
Do not add a promise of speed, legality or savings for the sake of appeal.

### 7. Generalizing openings
"Managing contractors across borders is complex"; «Работа с подрядчиками сложна».
If the next sentence already names the problem, delete the opening.
**Before:** «Документы сложно искать. Договоры хранятся в разных папках».
**After:** «Договоры хранятся в разных папках».
Do not invent a team size, geography, statistics or buyer behaviour.

### 8. Templated CTAs
Start today, Transform your workflow today; «Начните уже сегодня».
Name the available action: Book a demo, «Запросить демо», if such a path exists.
Do not remove a functional CTA together with the advertising lead-in.

## English text

### 9. Heavy function-word constructions
Complex connectives, extra articles and prepositions instead of a simple action.
Keep a grammatically necessary the; an article is not a marker in itself.

### 10. Not only… but also
Remove the artificial emphasis and state both independent facts directly.
If the contrast corrects a real misconception or distinguishes models, keep the distinction.
You must not lose approvals from "not only stores agreements but also records approvals".

### 11. Advertising rhetorical questions
Replace Are you struggling…?, Ready to unlock…? with the subject of the section.
Keep a real reader question or FAQ, for example "What documents are required?",
when the section answers it. Check the heading together with the answer.

### 12. Mechanically aligned lists
Equal length and superfluous parallel phrases can create a template.
The Oxford comma is a normal style choice, not a sign of AI. Leave a useful checklist as a list.

## Russian text

### 13. «Является» as a heavy copula
Often «это» or an ordinary nominal construction is enough.
In a definition, a legal statement or a quote the copula may be appropriate.

### 14. Officialese
«Осуществление контроля за выполнением» can be replaced with «контролирует выполнение».
Keep the subject of the action and the exact names of procedures.

### 15. «Данный»
In ordinary text «этот» usually fits better. In a quote or an exact document do not change it mechanically.

### 16. Superfluous introductory words
Remove «Стоит отметить», «Следует подчеркнуть» if the fact itself follows.
«Необходимо учитывать условие…» may contain a practical requirement: keep it.

### 17. «Широкий спектр», «ряд преимуществ»
Name the functions already listed or remove the vague intensifier.
Do not invent a missing quantity or example.

### 18. Decorative quotation marks
Frequent quotation marks around ordinary words and invented labels get in the way of reading.
Keep real quotes, names, the first introduction of a term and meaningful authorial irony.
One such turn does not determine the origin of the text.

### 19. Unnecessary anglicisms
Replace «workflow-менеджмент», «провижининг» with a clear Russian term where there is no need for them.
Keep established API, SaaS, SEO and professional terms.
In informational sections it is acceptable to explain payroll and other market categories;
the ban concerns unconfirmed statements about Example Co's capabilities, see the editorial policy.

### 20. «Не A, а B»
Remove an artificial contrast that rejects an opinion the reader never stated.
Keep real differences, negations and limits without a quantitative cap.
For example, the distinction between a services agreement and an employment contract carries meaning.

### 21. The dash as a universal link
If the dash replaces every type of link between parts, specify the link or split the sentence.
The obligatory Russian dash, a date range, a quote or authorial punctuation are acceptable.
There is no rigid norm of "one dash per N sentences".

## Additional EN/RU patterns

### 22. Excessive hedging
"could potentially possibly", «может, вероятно, в отдельных случаях» can often be shortened.
**Before:** "The change could potentially affect costs".
**After:** "The change may affect costs".
Keep may, usually, up to, approximately, «может», «обычно», «до», «около»
if they set the precision. Shortening must not turn a possibility into a guarantee.

### 23. Participial tails of significance
«…подчёркивая стремление к росту», "…further cementing its role" often add no fact.
Delete the tail; keep a real consequence with a source, as a separate sentence if needed.

### 24. Inflated significance
«Краеугольный камень», «играет ключевую роль», "pivotal turning point".
Name the specific change. Confirmed «первый», «единственный» and «впервые» ("first", "only",
"for the first time") may be facts: they must not be cut together with the pathos.

### 25. Inflated linking verbs
serves as, functions as; «выступает в качестве», «представляет собой».
Choose is or «это» when the meaning is the same. Keep the exact name of a role.

### 26. Vague attribution
«Эксперты считают», "Industry reports suggest" with no source named.
Use the source if it is given. If not, note the problem; do not make up a source
and do not turn the experts' opinion into your own established statement.

### 27. Artificial chattiness
«Но вот в чём дело», «И честно говоря», Here's the thing, Hot take.
Remove the staged turn before ordinary information.
Keep a real authorial remark in a fitting genre.

### 28. Out-of-place capitals after a colon
In a Russian enumeration «Преимущества: Скорость, Надёжность» usually needs lower case.
Keep names and abbreviations; sentence case does not cancel spelling rules.

### 29. Decorative bold and automatic conclusions
Do not highlight every line and do not add a "Main points" block that the task does not require.
User-requested key takeaways, useful labels in a table and a meaningful accent are acceptable.

### 30. An empty ending about the future
Remove «Несмотря на вызовы, будущее выглядит…» when it carries no new information.
Keep a confirmed plan, deadline or next step.

### 31. Uniform or deliberately choppy rhythm
Vary the construction where repetition gets in the way of reading. Do not fit sentences to
a numeric norm and do not chop the text into a telegraphic style for the sake of sounding human.
Parallel steps of an instruction may stay parallel.

### 32. Landscape metaphors
landscape, tapestry, journey, delve into; «в мире…», «богатая палитра».
In reference text speak about the subject directly. Keep literal and exact uses.

### 33. Overgeneralization
«Каждый предприниматель знает…» claims universality without grounds.
Keep the real scope of the source. Do not replace one invented audience with another.

### 34. Temporal openings
«В современном мире», «на сегодняшний день», «в условиях меняющейся реальности».
Remove the empty frame while keeping a meaningful date or the period for which a rule applies.

### 35. Mixed typography
Bring an unjustified mix of quotation marks to the style of the text.
An editor's automatic quotation marks do not prove AI; quotes and code are protected.

### 36. Forced triples
Check that every item stands on its own, especially in repeated cards.
Delete an empty repeat, not a real third item. For a checklist the number of items is set by
the task, not by an attempt to escape the "rule of three".

## Checking the structure of the whole page

### 37. Repeated endings and dramatic fragments
"That distinction matters", «Вот что действительно важно», a run of one-word sentences
after an explanation often only repeat the conclusion. Delete the repeat; keep a new consequence.
A short sentence with a new fact is acceptable.

### 38. Aphorisms instead of the subject
«Настоящий вопрос глубже», «документация — валюта доверия» mask an ordinary thesis.
State the thesis itself. Do not replace one slogan with another.

### 39. Staged lead-in
Let's dive in, Here's what you need to know; «Давайте разберёмся», «А теперь главное».
Remove the announcement of the coming explanation. Keep a navigation note that really
helps the reader.

### 40. Arguing with an invented objection
"Some might say… but", «Это не означает…» with no objection under discussion create noise.
Keep real alternatives, conditions and negations on which the reader's decision depends.

### 41. Self-description of the text and repeating the heading
"This section was compiled…", «Ниже мы рассмотрим…» and a first sentence that repeats the H2
delay the move to information. Remove the story about the preparation and the obvious layout.
Keep a meaningful reference to a source, a necessary explanation of a table or the methodology
in a section that really is devoted to methodology. Check neighbouring headings
and repeated cards in one reading.

### 42. Checking first sentences and openings
Read the first sentences of the paragraphs in a row, then separately the openings of list items
and cards. The first pass shows the development of the thought and repeats of the thesis; the
second shows empty frames such as a repeated «Это позволяет…» ("This allows…") before different facts.
Check the suspicious place against the full paragraph or item: the reader may need
the same form for steps, field names or comparable characteristics. The number of
identical openings does not set an automatic failure and does not require artificial
variety. Fix only an established loss of clarity or an empty repeat.

### 43. Technical leftovers from the draft
Check the user-facing text and metadata for service links and markers such as
`oai_citation`, `:contentReference[oaicite:`, `【1†source】`, `[cite: 1]`,
`[span_1]`, `sandbox:/mnt/data` and `</think>`. Before export this check may
be mechanical; it does not replace the meaning check or editorial QA.
In an ordinary article such leftovers are a preparation defect, not grounds for determining
authorship. If a marker occurs in the source, in code or in a verbatim quote, first
establish its purpose and the limits of the assignment. In a translation do not change the
immutable source and do not delete a protected quote to pass the check: report the conflict.
Do not delete invisible characters or URLs in bulk — some of them are legitimate elements.

## What does not help

- Shuffling synonyms without fixing the logic and structure.
- Typos, invented personal stories and forced sloppiness.
- Removing lists, qualifications, dashes or formal terms altogether.
- Judging quality by a single detector, by the length of the text or by the model's self-assessment.
- A second full rewrite without specific remarks on the first result.

## AI-Score assessment

Count **confirmed types of editorial defects**, not single markers.
Do not count protected quotes, useful constructions and the cases covered by the exceptions.
The score is needed for the report, not to establish authorship and not instead of the meaning check.

| Defect types | Score | Editorial interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0/10 | No patterns found; the text can stay without edits |
| 1–2 | 2–3/10 | Local edits |
| 3–4 | 4–5/10 | Several recurring formulas |
| 5–7 | 6–7/10 | The structure and wording need editing |
| 8+ | 8–10/10 | Many different templates; check the whole assigned material |

The scale is approximate: a lower score does not mean the text is more exact or better.
