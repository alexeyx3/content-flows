## A.2 "Yes, you can do it that way, but here is what may happen"

| Risk | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Agreements kept as email attachments | nobody can find the signed version when it is needed |
| Approvals in chat | an invoice is paid without sign-off |
| A spreadsheet as the contractor register | the register and reality diverge within a quarter |
| Copies of a template in personal folders | old clauses stay in circulation |
| No countersignature | the agreement does not say what the company thinks it says |
| IP | you cannot show that the work product was assigned to you |
| Reclassification | penalties and back taxes |
| Due diligence / M&A | a funding round or a sale slows down or falls through |
| Audit | sample requests go unanswered |
| Key person | the process leaves the company together with the employee |

## A.3 The understanding gap (details for content strategy)

- EOR — Employer of Record, AOR — Agent of Record, contractor management software: the audience confuses the three.
- 5–20 contractors: the founder handles the paperwork personally and folders with email are still enough; we explain what comes next and do not sell approvals here.
- 20+ contractors: finance takes over and the manual process starts to break — the first real need for invoice approval.
- 100+ contractors (several entities or countries): finance and operations split responsibilities, the founder no longer decides — approval steps and exports matter most.
- Clients often already have agreement templates from their own counsel and pay contractors through their bank; they do not see the gap between "we have agreements" and "we can show a signed agreement and an approved invoice for every contractor", and many take Example Co for a payment provider or an employer of record, which it is not.

## A.5 Objection handling

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| Globex / Initech / Hooli / Soylent also keep agreements and invoices in order? | They do it well, but they are built around employment and HR, and contractor paperwork is one module among many. We were built around the contractor record from the start — we know that work better and are more flexible on approval routes. |
| They are the market standard and you are not? | The standard of the broad market is not always the best fit for one job. If you need the whole employment and HR stack, the choice is obvious. If you need contractor agreements, invoices and approvals in one place without the rest — that is us. |
| Why do you not have feature X that Y has? | We deliberately do not build a universal HR platform. We concentrate on what affects the daily work of a finance and operations team with contractors in several countries. |
| Northwind / Contoso / Fabrikam / Umbrella / Vandelay are cheaper! | If the only criterion is the lowest price, there are cheaper options. If it matters that every invoice has a signed agreement and a named approver behind it, compare the document trail and the approval routes, not only the price. |
| What makes you better? | We do one thing: the contractor record — agreement, invoices, approvals — kept so that it can be handed to an accountant, an auditor or an investor as it is. We do not try to replace the HR stack or the accounting system. |
