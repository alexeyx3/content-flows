---
name: blog-topic-research
description: Researches a blog topic beyond the SERP using primary and independent sources and proposes concrete reader value before the brief.
---

Read job.yaml, semantics.csv, serp.serp_synthesis.md and knowledge/positioning.md,
icp-jtbd.md, blog-owned.md, article-enrichment.md. Output research.md in THIS job.
For jobs with `research_discovery_version: 1`, also read
`knowledge/gist-research.md` and output `research.discovery.md` before the
strategy brief. Follow that packet's four-heading contract.
For `research_v3_version: 1`, read `research_map.json` and use its Q-IDs.
Concentrate on official rules, reader questions, Reddit discovery and
qualitative original sources. The parallel `blog-study-research`,
`blog-primary-data-research` and `blog-quote-scout` own scholarly, document
and professional-voice searches. The discovery packet still has four
headings, but its search log needs only the primary lane; name the sibling
packets for scholarly/social results rather than inventing search rows.
For jobs with `primary_data_research_version: 1`, a separate specialist
searches original documents and numeric datasets in parallel. Do not wait for
`research.data.json` or duplicate its systematic file-type search. Record
important numerical leads found incidentally in `research.md` for the brief;
the data specialist's packet and evidence agent determine publishable values.

Investigate the reader's actual decision, not just the named vendors. Search for
primary data, government/standards sources, independent studies and authoritative
industry reports. Read the sources; search snippets alone do not establish a fact.
For the new discovery protocol, formulate the reader decision and missing nodes
first. Run bounded question-specific searches in an academic catalogue
(OpenAlex or Crossref) and indexed professional discussion (including LinkedIn
when relevant), recording queries and unsuccessful or irrelevant results. No
study or social post is required in the article merely because it was found.
This catalogue/discussion search instruction applies only when
`research_v3_version: 1` is absent; v3 dispatches dedicated parallel agents.
Never scrape LinkedIn or lift a quotation from a search snippet. A social post
can identify a question to verify; a direct external quote is only a candidate
when exact wording, authorship, date and original source are verifiable through
an authorized readable source. Keep outside voices distinct from our author's
editorial comments.
Use company cards to avoid repeating already verified work, but keep topical
research independent of a vendor's marketing claims. Reuse sibling research only
where the source, jurisdiction and date still fit this topic.

research.md contains:

1. Reader questions and what existing answers leave unresolved.
2. Findings with stable IDs R-01, R-02…; finding, exact supporting passage,
   source URL, source tier, checked date, jurisdiction, limitations.
3. Information Gain candidates: concrete reader benefit, findings used and why it
   adds to the SERP. Practical synthesis/decision framework is allowed, clearly
   distinguish it from original data or a test you actually performed.
4. Visual candidates: what a chart or diagram would explain, available data and
   missing values; no invented numbers. Suggest useful code visuals only.
5. Expert-comment questions: a specific decision or trade-off the author/editor
   can explain; list any provided first-hand material separately.
6. Gaps and needs_manual_confirmation. On blocked sources, mark the limitation
   and find corroboration; do not present a search extraction as a read source.

Read audience.reddit.md when present as discovery, never as evidence. This is a
pre-brief research stage; do not spend it on full vendor cards or article writing.
The four SERP markets are a discovery sample, not the article's geographic scope.
Research jurisdictions only where their distinctions help the reader's decision;
use fewer or more countries, or a supported regional frame where appropriate.
Do not add US/GB/DE/AU findings merely to cover the sampled markets. Preserve the
actual scope of each source; never generalize a national legal rule to the EU/world.

Aim for a few strong findings rather than a quota of links. If no useful new
finding emerges, report blocked and explain the gap to the orchestrator.
blog-evidence will verify and convert factual claims into evidence.md rows before
the writer sees them. Final message: IDs of useful findings, value candidates,
visual candidates and unresolved gaps; for new jobs, state scholarly and social
search outcomes. No full article draft.
