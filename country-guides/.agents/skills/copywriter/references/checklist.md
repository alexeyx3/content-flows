# Example Co wording check — the product rules

The short pre-handoff check. It replaces any longer «words to avoid» glossary: one list, kept in step with the product card.
Full rules: `knowledge/product-positioning.md` («Naming & category», §1) and `knowledge/guides-rules.md` section 2.

## Use

- Category formula, in full at most once per page: «software for companies that work with independent contractors abroad: agreements, invoices and approvals in one place». Then short variants in turn: contractor agreements, contractor paperwork, work with contractors abroad.
- Product name: Example Workspace.
- Client side: companies keep contractor agreements and approve contractor invoices in Example Workspace. In a country guide, both sides: «companies can sign agreements with and approve invoices from independent contractors in <Country> in Example Co, and contractors based in <Country> can work with clients abroad».
- Approvals: invoice approval / approvals / sign-off, in turn; with «invoice» already in the sentence, just «approval».
- Who uses it: finance and operations teams; in a small company, the founder who handles contractor paperwork personally.
- Documents and exports, each at most once per page: «agreements from templates, e-signature and a document trail per contractor»; «exports to accounting software».
- Coverage, at most once per page: «contractors in 60+ countries».
- Price, only where the text describes price, verbatim: «from $25 per active contractor per month, charged only for months with an approved invoice; no setup fee».
- Risk: «helps keep contractor documents in order».
- Agent of Record: only to describe how a market arrangement works («under an agent of record arrangement a third party signs the agreements and collects the invoices on the company's behalf»).
- The one permitted negation: «Example Co is not an employer of record».

## Never next to Example Co

- «Example Co works with contractors in <Country>».
- Labels: staffing agency, recruiter, marketplace, employer of record, law firm, legal adviser, payment provider, payment platform, HR system; «freelancer management system», «contractor compliance platform», «global workforce platform».
- Speed: instant, same-day, in minutes, fast. At most «in a few steps».
- Legal certification: «certified», «notarised» or «legally verified» agreements; a seal or stamp next to e-signature. Write «signed with e-signature».
- «Cheap», «free», «low-cost», «subscription» or «per seat» for the price.
- A product for the contractor alone; «HR software».
- Guarantees: audit-proof, fully compliant, risk-free, guaranteed compliance.
- Onboarding as a function. Write «collecting the contractor's details and a signed agreement before work starts».
- A list of regions or countries in place of «contractors in 60+ countries».
- Superlatives without a public source (best, leading, number one); customer counts or growth figures not published on the site.
- Roadmap and absent features; conversational or hedged tone.
- A brand listed in `config/stop-brands.json` — anywhere on the page.
