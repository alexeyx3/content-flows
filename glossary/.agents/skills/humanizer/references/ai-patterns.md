# AI patterns in Example Co texts

A catalogue of editorial defects for EN and RU. The presence of a construction does not prove
AI authorship. The priorities from SKILL.md apply to all examples below.

## How to apply the catalogue

- First determine what gets in the reader's way: an empty lead-in, repetition, an unclear link,
  an unfounded promise. Do not treat a match with the vocabulary as sufficient grounds.
- Weak signals (dashes, commas, passive voice, formal words, uniform length) are to be
  judged in context. Do not fix them automatically and do not attribute authorship.
- Numbers, real items, conditions, caveats, negations, the actor and the division of
  responsibilities are preserved. "Helps" does not equal "does it for the client".
- The examples show a way of editing. They give no new facts about Example Co.
  Specifics are taken only from the source text or the facts provided.
- If the exact fact is missing, shorten the formula or note the question in the report.
  Do not replace "quickly" with an invented "24 hours", or "may" with a proven result.
- Quotes, names, technical terms, URLs and protected blocks are not cleaned by vocabulary.

## Universal patterns

### 1. Structural symmetry
Repeated grammar and equal length of statements create rhythm instead of content.
Keep three genuine functions or three conditions. Do not remove an item for the sake of a number.
Check paragraphs and cards as well, not only sentences.

### 2. Empty intensifiers
seamlessly, effortlessly, powerful, cutting-edge, game-changing; «бесшовно»,
«передовой», «комплексный» without elaboration. Delete the word if it specifies nothing.
robust, reliable and other terms in an exact technical sense may be needed.

### 3. Blurred agency
helps, allows, enables, empowers; «позволяет», «помогает», «даёт возможность».
Specify the real action, keeping who performs it.
**Before:** «The platform helps teams manage records by storing agreements together.»
**After:** «The platform stores agreements together so teams can manage the records.»
This sentence must not turn into «The platform manages the contractors for you».

### 4. Nominalizations instead of action
A pile-up of -tion/-ment and «обеспечение автоматизации оптимизации» hides the action.
**Before:** «Платформа обеспечивает автоматизацию документооборота».
**After:** «Платформа автоматизирует документооборот».
Do not replace a legal name or an exact term with a colloquial approximate word.

### 5. Meta-comments
In summary, It's worth noting, This means that; «Важно отметить», «Подводя итог».
Remove the announcement when the information can be given directly. Keep the causal link,
condition or necessary conclusion: the words «таким образом» are not always empty.

### 6. Template and vague headings
Seamless Contractor Management for Global Teams; «Эффективное решение для бизнеса».
Name the subject of the section and check that it matches the content.
**Before:** «Unlock seamless contractor onboarding».
**After:** «Contractor onboarding steps», if the section really contains steps.
Do not add a promise of speed, legality or savings for the sake of appeal.

### 7. Generalizing openers
«Managing contractors across borders is complex»; «Работа с подрядчиками сложна».
If the next sentence already names the problem, delete the opener.
**Before:** «Документы сложно искать. Договоры хранятся в разных папках».
**After:** «Договоры хранятся в разных папках».
Do not invent team size, geography, statistics or buyer behaviour.

### 8. Template CTAs
Start today, Transform your workflow today; «Начните уже сегодня».
Name the available action: Book a demo, «Запросить демо», if such a path exists.
Do not remove a functional CTA together with the promotional lead-in.

## English text

### 9. Heavy function constructions
Complex connectives, extra articles and prepositions instead of a simple action.
Keep a grammatically necessary "the"; an article by itself is not a marker.

### 10. Not only… but also
Remove the artificial emphasis and name both independent facts directly.
If the contrast corrects a real misconception or distinguishes models, keep the distinction.
"not only stores agreements but also records approvals" must not lose approvals.

### 11. Promotional rhetorical questions
Are you struggling…?, Ready to unlock…? are replaced with the subject of the section.
Keep a real reader question or FAQ, for example "What documents are required?",
when the section answers it. Check the heading together with the answer.

### 12. Mechanically aligned lists
Equal length and extra parallel turns of phrase can create a template.
The Oxford comma is a normal style choice, not a sign of AI. Keep a useful checklist as a list.

## Russian text

### 13. «Является» as a heavy linking verb
Often «это» or a plain nominal construction is enough.
In a definition, a legal statement or a quote the linking verb may be appropriate.

### 14. Bureaucratese
«Осуществление контроля за выполнением» can be replaced with «контролирует выполнение».
Keep the subject of the action and the exact names of procedures.

### 15. «Данный»
In ordinary text «этот» usually fits better. Do not change it mechanically in a quote or an exact document.

### 16. Redundant introductory words
Remove «Стоит отметить», «Следует подчеркнуть» if the fact itself follows.
«Необходимо учитывать условие…» may contain a practical requirement: keep it.

### 17. «Широкий спектр», «ряд преимуществ»
Name the functions already listed or remove the vague intensifier.
Do not invent a missing quantity or example.

### 18. Decorative quotation marks
Frequent quotation marks around ordinary words and invented labels hinder reading.
Keep real quotes, names, the first introduction of a term and meaningful authorial irony.
A single such turn does not determine the origin of a text.

### 19. Unnecessary anglicisms
Replace «spend-management», «провижининг» with a clear Russian term where not necessary.
Keep established API, SaaS, SEO and professional terms.
In informational sections it is acceptable to explain payroll and other market categories;
the ban applies to unconfirmed claims about Example Co's functions, see the editorial policy.

### 20. «Не A, а B»
Remove an artificial contrast that rejects an opinion the reader has not stated.
Keep real differences, negations and limitations, with no numerical limit.
For example, distinguishing a services contract from an employment contract carries meaning.

### 21. The dash as a universal link
If the dash replaces every type of link between parts, specify the link or split the sentence.
A mandatory Russian dash, a date range, a quote or authorial punctuation are acceptable.
There is no hard norm of "one dash per N sentences".

## Additional EN/RU patterns

### 22. Excessive hedging
«could potentially possibly», «может, вероятно, в отдельных случаях» can often be shortened.
**Before:** «The change could potentially affect costs».
**After:** «The change may affect costs».
Keep may, usually, up to, approximately, «может», «обычно», «до», «около»
if they set precision. Shortening must not turn a possibility into a guarantee.

### 23. Participial significance tails
«…подчёркивая стремление к росту», «…further cementing its role» often add no fact.
Delete the tail; keep a real consequence with a source, in a separate sentence if necessary.

### 24. Inflated significance
«Краеугольный камень», «играет ключевую роль», «pivotal turning point».
Name the concrete change. Confirmed "first", "only" and "for the first time"
may be facts: they must not be cut together with the pathos.

### 25. Inflated linking verbs
serves as, functions as; «выступает в качестве», «представляет собой».
Choose is or «это» when the meaning is the same. Keep the exact name of a role.

### 26. Vague attribution
«Эксперты считают», «Industry reports suggest» without naming the source.
Use the source if it is given. If not, note the problem; do not invent a source
and do not turn experts' opinion into your own established claim.

### 27. Artificial conversational tone
«Но вот в чём дело», «И честно говоря», Here's the thing, Hot take.
Remove the staged turn before ordinary information.
Keep a real authorial remark in a suitable genre.

### 28. Inappropriate capitals after a colon
In a Russian enumeration «Преимущества: Скорость, Надёжность» lowercase is usually required.
Keep names and abbreviations; sentence case does not cancel orthography.

### 29. Decorative bold and automatic conclusions
Do not highlight every line and do not add a «Главное» block that the task does not require.
User-specified key takeaways, useful labels in a table and a meaningful emphasis are acceptable.

### 30. Empty closing about the future
Remove «Несмотря на вызовы, будущее выглядит…» without new information.
Keep a confirmed plan, deadline or next step.

### 31. Uniform or deliberately choppy rhythm
Vary the construction where repetition hinders reading. Do not fit sentences to
a numerical norm and do not chop the text into a telegraphic style for the sake of a human sound.
Parallel steps of an instruction may stay parallel.

### 32. Landscape metaphors
landscape, tapestry, journey, delve into; «в мире…», «богатая палитра».
In reference text, speak about the subject directly. Keep literal and exact uses.

### 33. Overgeneralization
«Каждый предприниматель знает…» claims universality without grounds.
Keep the real scale of the source. Do not replace one invented audience with another.

### 34. Time-based openers
«В современном мире», «на сегодняшний день», «в условиях меняющейся реальности».
Remove the empty frame, keeping a meaningful date or the period in which a rule applies.

### 35. Mixed typography
Bring an unjustified mix of quotation marks to the style of the text.
The editor's automatic quotes do not prove AI; quotes and code are protected.

### 36. Forced triples
Check the independence of each item, especially in repeated cards.
Remove empty repetition, not a real third item. For a checklist the number of items is determined
by the task, not by an attempt to escape the "rule of three".

## Whole-page structure check

### 37. Repeated endings and dramatic fragments
«That distinction matters», «Вот что действительно важно», a series of one-word sentences
after an explanation often merely repeat the conclusion. Remove the repetition; keep a new consequence.
A short sentence with a new fact is acceptable.

### 38. Aphorisms instead of the subject
«Настоящий вопрос глубже», «документация — валюта доверия» mask an ordinary thesis.
Express the thesis itself. Do not replace one slogan with another.

### 39. Staged lead-in
Let's dive in, Here's what you need to know; «Давайте разберёмся», «А теперь главное».
Remove the announcement of an upcoming explanation. Keep a navigation clarification that genuinely
helps the reader.

### 40. Arguing with an invented objection
«Some might say… but», «Это не означает…» without an objection actually under discussion create noise.
Keep real alternatives, conditions and negations on which the reader's decision depends.

### 41. Text self-description and heading repetition
«This section was compiled…», «Ниже мы рассмотрим…» and a first sentence that repeats the H2
delay the move to the information. Remove the story about preparation and the obvious layout.
Keep a meaningful reference to a source, a necessary explanation of a table, or a methodology
in a section that is really devoted to methodology. Check neighbouring headings
and repeated cards in a single reading.

## What does not help

- Swapping synonyms without fixing the logic and structure.
- Typos, invented personal stories and forced sloppiness.
- Complete removal of lists, caveats, dashes or formal terms.
- Judging quality by a single detector, text length or the model's self-assessment.
- A second full rewrite without specific remarks on the first result.

## AI-Score assessment

Count **confirmed types of editorial defects**, not individual markers.
Do not count protected quotes, useful constructions and cases covered by the exceptions.
The score is for the report, not for establishing authorship and not in place of a check of meaning.

| Defect types | Score | Editorial interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0/10 | No patterns found; the text may stay without edits |
| 1–2 | 2–3/10 | Local edits |
| 3–4 | 4–5/10 | Several repeating formulas |
| 5–7 | 6–7/10 | Structure and wording need editing |
| 8+ | 8–10/10 | Many different templates; check all the given material |

The scale is approximate: a lower score does not mean the text is more accurate or better.
