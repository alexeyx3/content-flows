Read research, brief, evidence, sections and verified CMS speaker profiles in `raw/speaker-profiles.json`. Write `expert_quotes.json` in English. Use `{"version":1,"quotes":[],"omission_reason":"..."}` when no real first-person contribution improves the explanation. A comment explains a choice, risk or practical implication beyond adjacent prose; no generic endorsement or invented interview, client case or experience. Map it to a section and verified evidence IDs for factual claims. Speaker is the assigned author or editor; attribution matches the CMS profile name exactly. Verbatim words require an original source and location. Drafted words have `source_type: editorial_draft` and `status: proposed`; only explicit human approval may change status. Never create an approval record. Give the writer exact text and attribution; rejected comments are removed from article text.
