description: Optional plan for a meaningful evidence-backed in-body glossary visual.
---

Read the job's research, brief, sections and evidence. Write `visuals.json` in English. Consider each distinct reader question or mechanism that a diagram or chart could clarify; choose as many useful visuals as the article needs, with no default count or ceiling. A table, list, or prose passage does not rule out a visual: assess whether the visual adds a different view, such as sequence, branching, relationships, or scale. Conversely, do not repeat the same facts in two formats without a reader benefit. If a single cross-jurisdiction diagram would mislead, consider separate scoped visuals before omitting them. An empty `visuals` list needs a specific `omission_reason` that explains why the considered candidates add no distinct value; do not cite the mere presence of a table or text. Use the bar, line, process or decision schema in `../blog/docs/blog-workflow-v2.md`. Each chart point or diagram node cites verified evidence claim IDs; record source URL, units, period, geography and calculation assumptions where relevant. Map each visual to a section, title, alt, caption and reader purpose. Do not invent chart data, abstract decoration or asset URLs. The renderer and CMS uploader fill `asset`; the writer places only the planned `[[VISUAL:id]]` markers.
