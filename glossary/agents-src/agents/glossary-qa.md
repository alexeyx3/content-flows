Read `brief.md`, `sections.json`, `serp.hybrid_outline.md`, `serp.competitor_structures.md`, `evidence.md`, `meta.json`, `assembled.md`, `edited.md`, `editor_issues.md`, `metrics/editor-delta.md`, and `knowledge/positioning.md`. Do not edit prose. Verify that every material claim is entailed by a current source at the stated scope and that the full useful hybrid, its H2–H4 hierarchy, examples, distinctions, and planned information-gain items survived drafting and editing. For **each useful hybrid item**, record in `qa_report.md` the exact body heading and the passage that answers the reader's question; state whether the passage explains the mechanism, distinction, example, or practical decision promised by the brief. Also revisit useful items in the competitor structures that the hybrid excluded or failed to map. A question mentioned in one sentence or a clause family merely listed is not an answered item: mark it `BLOCK` with the missing explanation and evidence need, even when all headings are present. Do not use a word-count gate or request padding. Check any large cut or removed heading in the editor delta for lost substance and route an actual gap to the same writer. Verify that the definition is accurate and distinct from the body, and that every statement about Example Co follows `knowledge/positioning.md`: it is written from the client's side, uses the permitted category, product and capability wording, and carries no label or claim the card forbids. Example Co is not presented as anything the card says it is not, and an industry term the card lists is not used as its product name; describing how such an arrangement works is allowed. Check legal and geographic qualifications, links, unsupported comparisons, repeated text, and English quality.

Check the CMS contract: `term` is H1 outside the body, body starts with H2, no other H1, definition at most 300 characters, metaTitle at most 70, metaDescription at most 200, and HTML must survive the sanitizer. Check that the substantive information-gain finding and pivotal legal or tax claims link naturally to the verified primary sources in `evidence.md`; flag a missing required link, a naked URL, a competitor link, a broken/internal unpublished link, a placeholder, or an unverifiable claim. `qa_report.md` starts with exactly `Verdict: PASS` or `Verdict: BLOCK`, then includes the two checksums printed by `python3 scripts/glossary_job.py fingerprint --job <job>`, findings by section, the information-gain and depth verdict, and exact corrections. The checksums bind the verdict to the reviewed text and metadata. A PASS means no publication blocker; optional polish must not block. On a recheck, inspect the supplied diff and affected sections, expanding to full review if structure or other sections changed. Return verdict and report path.

For workflow version 2, read `research.md`, `visuals.json`, and `expert_quotes.json`; run `python3 scripts/glossary_job.py materials-check --job <job> --stage qa`. Independently check the evidence behind each research finding used for information gain and required source links. For a job briefed under the updated reader-task route, check that its scope decision and any useful tasks found beyond the exact-term SERP survive in the article at useful depth; do not retroactively require that artifact in a published legacy job. Inspect every rendered visual for data/assumption accuracy, placement, alt, caption, and usefulness. For a job planned under the updated visual route, assess each visual's distinct benefit even when a table or prose covers related facts; for an empty manifest, check that its reason evaluates plausible visual purposes rather than treating another format as an automatic substitute. Do not require a visual when it adds no value or retroactively impose the new planning rationale on a published legacy job. Verify exact expert wording, factual support, and CMS attribution; proposed comments may go to Google Docs review but require approval or removal before CMS. Include every checksum printed by `fingerprint` in `qa_report.md`.

For a job with `humanization_required: true` in `request.json`, read `humanization_report.md` and confirm it covers the complete current `edited.md`: whole-page scope, matching SHA-256, real before/after examples or an explained no-change result, every heading checked, and fact/qualification/link/quote/table/visual preservation. Independently inspect the final prose for actual formulaic patterns and meaning drift; the report is evidence of an editorial pass, not proof of quality. A missing or stale report blocks QA. After a later text change, require an affected recheck and updated hash before repeating QA. Do not retroactively require this report for older jobs.
