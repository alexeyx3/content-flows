## Shared writing rules

The separate `definition` appears before the body. Before drafting a unit, identify its reader question, content contract in `brief.md`, and mapped useful points in `serp.hybrid_outline.md`, including accepted reader tasks found beyond the exact-term SERP. Write the requested unit fully, usefully, and naturally. Go deeper than the inspected competing pages where evidence permits. Give the reader the explanation, distinctions, examples, and practical implications promised by the full hybrid outline; do not stop after merely naming a topic. Start with the answer, then develop it without padding, repeating the definition or prior units, or using formulaic introductions. Use only facts marked `supports` in `evidence.md`, or carefully scoped facts marked `partial` within their recorded limits. An example needs the same evidence discipline as any other factual claim. Link the substantive information-gain finding and pivotal legal or tax claims naturally to their verified primary-source URLs from `evidence.md`; do not use naked URLs or link to competing vendors. Do not cite the same source repeatedly without a distinct reader need. When evidence is inadequate for a useful planned point, report the gap rather than filling it from memory or silently omitting the point. Preserve natural English and Example Co's educational voice. There is no length target or ceiling. FAQ, tables, lists, and H4s follow reader need and the approved outline; no automatic CTA or Key Takeaways.

Before handing off a unit, reread its content contract and mapped hybrid points against the draft. If a promised distinction, mechanism, example, or practical decision is merely named or listed, develop the answer in this same turn. If evidence prevents that, report the exact gap instead of claiming the unit is complete.

Place each planned visual's exact `[[VISUAL:id]]` marker after the explanatory passage in its assigned section; never invent an image URL. A planned diagram may complement a table when each has a distinct explanatory job. Include an expert comment only if it adds practical first-person value, using the exact text and attribution in the manifest as a blockquote. Proposed text remains proposed through review. Do not invent experience or use an expert opinion as evidence.
