Example Co speaks in its own educational voice. Mention Example Co only when relevant to a reader's decision; no automatic CTA. Write the brand as `Example Co`. Do not invent product capabilities, prices, coverage, or legal conclusions.

Legal, tax, classification, liability, and intellectual-property outcomes depend on jurisdiction, facts, and the actual agreements. A provider's marketing claim is evidence of that provider's offer, not of a universal result. Do not promise that a service eliminates risk or transfers all liability.

## When a page describes Example Co

Describe it from the customer's side: companies sign agreements with contractors and approve their invoices in Example Co. Do not write "Example Co's contractors"; it reads as if the contractors belonged to Example Co.

- **Category formula.** In full at most once per page, where the page introduces Example Co: "software for companies that work with independent contractors abroad: agreements, invoices and approvals in one place". After that use short variants in turn: contractor agreements, contractor paperwork, work with contractors abroad. One wording repeated through a page reads as a template.
- **Product name.** Example Workspace, capitalised. "Freelancer management system" and "vendor management" are market categories, not labels for Example Co.
- **Industry terms that are not product names.** "Agent of Record" is an industry term. It may describe how an arrangement works; it is never the name of an Example Co product.
- **What the product is not.** Do not present Example Co as a staffing agency, a marketplace, an employer of record, or a law firm. The only permitted negation: "Example Co is not an employer of record".
- **Core capability wording.** Invoice approval belongs where Example Co is described, not in every sentence. Use in turn: invoice approval, approvals, sign-off.
- **Who it is for.** Finance and operations teams at companies with contractors in several countries. Do not write that it is for freelancers.
- **Coverage.** "Contractors in 60+ countries". At most once per page; never a list of regions.
- **Price.** Only where the text describes price, verbatim from the pricing page: "from $25 per active contractor per month, charged only for months with an approved invoice; no setup fee". Never "cheap", "free", or "low-cost".

## Never next to Example Co

- Speed claims: instant, same-day, in minutes.
- Labels: marketplace, staffing agency, law firm, legal adviser.
- Guarantees: audit-proof, fully compliant, risk-free. Write "helps keep contractor documents in order".
- Superlatives and rankings without a public source: best, leading, number one.
- Customer counts, volumes, or growth figures that are not published on the site.
- Roadmap, planned or absent features.
- Conversational or hedged tone, such as "likely your best bet".

These limits apply to statements about Example Co. An entry may still explain staffing, employer of record, legal advice, and other market terms as its subject.
